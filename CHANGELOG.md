# Changelog

## 0.3.3 — 2026-09-20

First public source release, under the MIT license.

- Multiple inboxes with shared or local rules and Review first controls.
- Redesigned menu panel, settings, activity history, and per-file action plans.
- Local OCR, editable document details, searchable PDFs, archive search, and saved views.
- Exact duplicate finder with a copy-to-keep choice, review before Trash, and Undo.
- Optional email attachment intake and model assistance with explicit automatic-use permission.
- Archive integrity checks, original preservation, and scan splitting.
- English starter rules, no preconfigured services, and Review first enabled for new installations.
- Setup, rule, privacy, integration, command-line, and contributor guides; macOS CI.

Existing configurations are preserved. Installation is from source; notarized downloads,
SSH/SFTP connections, and stale-file suggestions are not included in this release.
